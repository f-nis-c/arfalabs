Arsalaan Hasan Shaikh, Falak Nishant Chalasany

WHAT IT DOES
Schedules processes with the algorithm you choose and prints a timeline,
a table of times and the averages. "All" compares every algorithm.

RUN
  python3 cp.py
Procs: 4
Enter id at bt pri (low pri = first):
a 0 8 2
b 1 4 1
c 2 9 4
d 3 5 3
Pick: 3

INPUT
id   a name       at   arrival time    bt   burst time
pri  priority, a lower number runs first
Times are whole numbers. Round Robin and All ask for a quantum.

MENU
1 FCFS   2 SJF   3 SRTF   4 Pri   5 PriP (preemptive)   6 RR   7 All

HOW TO READ THE OUTPUT
Timeline  |a 0-8 |b 8-12| means a ran from 0 to 8, then b.
CT   completion time
TAT  turnaround time = CT - AT
WT   waiting time    = TAT - BT
Avg  mean TAT and WT. Lower is better.
